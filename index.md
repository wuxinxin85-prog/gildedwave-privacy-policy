# Privacy Policy – Gilded Wave

Last updated: 2026-11-05

Gilded Wave is a watch face for Wear OS. It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- Heart rate, step count, battery level, sunrise/sunset and weather are read on your watch only in order to display them on the watch face. This data is never uploaded, transmitted or shared with the developer or any third party.
- The watch face does not use advertising, analytics or tracking.
- The app requests the BODY_SENSORS permission (heart rate) and the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission (steps) solely for displaying these values on the watch face.
